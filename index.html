Xin Fan

I am a PhD student at Waseda University’s Graduate School of Fundamental Science and Engineering, working in Yamana Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Hayato Yamana.

My research centers on large language models.

  • Past research: pre-training data detection and memorization evaluation.
  • Ongoing interests: knowledge editing, machine unlearning, test-time adaptation, foundation model interpretability, spatial understanding and reasoning, and world models.
  • Current research: inference acceleration and safe generation.
